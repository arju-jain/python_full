Privacy Policy

At Aura Luxe, privacy is fundamental to luxury. We respect your confidentiality and safeguard all personal data using industry-standard protocols.

1. Information Collection

We collect your email, delivery address, and transaction metadata strictly to process orders and provide client concierge services. We do not store sensitive payment card information.

2. Vendor Information Sharing

Vendors receive only the delivery recipient details necessary to dispatch their specific fulfilled items. No vendor is granted visibility into purchases made with other vendors in the same cart.

3. Data Security

All sessions, passwords, and cryptographic checkout tokens utilize PBKDF2 hashing, secure CSRF mitigation, and TLS 1.3 transport encryption.
